About us

FAQ

Before you write to us

Can't find your question? Write to us: we answer every one.

I don't speak Italian. Is that a problem?+

No. We work with you in English and handle the Italian paperwork and communication with Italian bodies on your behalf. EU applications themselves are usually written in English.

What are "direct" EU funds?+

Grants managed directly by the European Commission and its agencies, not by Italian Regions or Ministries. Applications compete at European level: fewer Italian companies apply and grants are often larger.

Can my company apply?+

Companies registered in Italy can access many direct calls. Eligibility and partnership requirements vary by programme: we check them for free.

Do I need a fully defined project?+

No. A concrete idea is enough: we shape it together around the requirements of the call.

Does the grant cover all costs?+

Often not: many calls require co-financing from your company. We tell you clearly before we start.

Are you linked to the EU institutions?+

No. We are independent consultants with no affiliation to the institutions of the European Union.
