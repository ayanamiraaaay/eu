Privacy and cookie notice

Last updated: 3 November 2026. The Italian version of this notice prevails.

Data controller

LazioEU. For any request about your data: studiompfinancial@gmail.com.

Cookies

This website uses no cookies, technical or profiling, and no analytics or tracking tools. That is why there is no cookie banner.

What data we process

Browsing. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.), which logs technical data such as your IP address for security and operation. Fonts are served by Bunny Fonts (BunnyWay d.o.o., Slovenia, EU), which receives your IP address to deliver them to your browser.

Assessment requests. The contact form does not send or store any data on this website: it opens your email app with a prefilled message, which you decide whether to send. If you write to us, we process the data you give us (name, email, phone if provided, project description) only to reply and prepare the assessment you asked for. Our mailbox is provided by Google (Gmail).

Legal basis and retention

We process request data to take pre-contractual steps at your request (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR). We keep it for as long as needed to handle your request; if no engagement follows, we delete it, unless the law requires otherwise.

Your rights

You can ask at any time for access, rectification, erasure, restriction of or objection to processing, and portability of your data (Arts. 15–22 GDPR) by writing to the address above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with the Italian Data Protection Authority, the Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).
